Event log

    Why this is a digital twin
    1 · Physical entity
    Your real hand.
    2 · Sensor
    Phone camera + on-device ML turns video into 21 3D joint positions.
    3 · Data link
    WebSocket stream, ~30 updates/sec, through a cloud relay in a private room.
    4 · Virtual model
    The skeleton: a live copy of the hand's state.
    5 · Intelligence
    Fingers up → gesture → action (draw / clear).
    6 · Feedback loop
    Gesture, color and vibration sent back to the phone.
    7 · Monitoring
    Speed, steadiness, link health and gesture time, live.
    8 · History
    Record a session and replay it - the twin re-lives the past.
    9 · Simulation
    A virtual hand drives the same twin with no hardware.
    10 · Prediction
    Forecasts the fingertip ahead in time and measures its own accuracy.
    11 · Multiple assets
    The phone itself has a twin too: its orientation is mirrored in 3D, classified (flat, upright…) and a shake clears the canvas.
