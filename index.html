    3 · Data link
    WebSocket stream, ~30 updates/sec, through a cloud relay in a private room.

    The skeleton: a live copy of the hand's state.

    Fingers up → gesture → action (draw / clear).

    Record a session and replay it - the twin re-lives the past.

    A virtual hand drives the same twin with no hardware.

    Forecasts the fingertip ahead in time and measures its own accuracy.

    Why this is a digital twin
    Scenario
    The phone is the sensor tag on a fragile parcel (lab samples, glassware, electronics) in transit.
    1 · Physical entity
    The parcel - represented by your phone.
    2 · Sensors
    Gyroscope + accelerometer: orientation (α, β, γ), shocks and free fall, 20×/sec.
    3 · Virtual model
    The 3D parcel follows the real one in real time.
    4 · Rules & intelligence
    The twin checks every reading against the shipping rules and detects tilts, flips, shocks and drops.
    5 · Monitoring & history
    Condition score, live charts and an incident timeline.
    6 · Feedback loop
    The phone shows the parcel status and vibrates on every incident.
    7 · Simulation
    “Simulate delivery trip” replays a realistic trip with no hardware.
    8 · Reporting
    A delivery report proves how the parcel was handled.
