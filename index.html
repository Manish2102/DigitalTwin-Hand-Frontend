          How to present
          1. Turn Gestures on and allow the camera. Stand where the preview shows your hand.
          2. Open your hand and move it sideways in one quick, deliberate sweep: right for the next slide, left for the previous one. Wait for the green flash before the next swipe.
          3. Normal talking movements, a closed hand, slow drifts and up-down movements are ignored. If slides still change by accident, choose Careful.
          4. Keys always work: → ← Space change slides, F full screen, B black screen, G gestures, C camera preview.

            Teach it your signs

            For a presentation: record each sign you will use 3 times, especially Welcome (it is not in the built-in vocabulary, so it only works once you teach it). It then matches your way of signing. The recordings stay in this browser.

            Start the camera first.
            For good recognition
            1. Sit or stand about 1 to 1.5 m from the camera so your head, shoulders and both hands are in view.
            2. Use even light on your face and hands; avoid a bright window behind you.
            3. Words: start with your hands down, sign one word, then lower your hands. Letters: hold each one still.
            4. If it is not sure, it says so and offers suggestions. Tap one to add it, or sign again.

            How it works and its limits
            Physical entity
            Any object you hold up to the camera.
            Sensor
            The webcam takes one photo. The photo never leaves this computer.
            Virtual model
            An AI model guesses the depth of every pixel, and the page turns it into a solid mesh coloured with your photo.
            A snapshot twin
            Unlike the hand and parcel twins, this copy does not update live: it records how the object looked at one moment. Saved scans can be exported.
            Limits
            Depth is estimated, not measured, so it is relative. Only the front is real; the back is flat and the sides are guesses. Shiny, see-through and furry objects work badly. Use good light. Enter the real width to get a true size.
