      Why this is a digital twin
      Scenario
      The phone is the sensor tag on a fragile parcel (lab samples, glassware, electronics) in transit.
      Sensors
      Gyroscope and accelerometer: orientation, shocks and free fall, 20 times per second.
      Virtual model
      The 3D parcel follows the real one in real time.
      Rules & intelligence
      Every reading is checked against the shipping rules to detect tilts, flips, shocks and drops.
      Monitoring & history
      Condition score, live charts and an incident timeline.
      Feedback loop
      The phone shows the parcel status and vibrates on every incident.
      Simulation & reporting
      A scripted trip runs without hardware; the delivery report proves how the parcel was handled.
